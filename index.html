🧪 Experimental Investigation

🔬 Central Research Question

How does physical exertion (e.g., 2 minutes of intense jumping jacks/running on spot) affect the visual-motor reaction time of human subjects?

🧠 Biological & Physiological Hypothesis

Physical exercise elevates heart rate and oxygenated blood circulation to the primary motor cortex. We hypothesize that moderate physical exertion will decrease reaction time due to enhanced neural signal transmission speed across synapses. However, severe physical fatigue may reverse this effect.

⚡ Visual-Motor Circuit Pathway

Photoreceptors in Retina ➔ Optic Nerve ➔ Visual Cortex (Occipital) ➔ Motor Cortex (Frontal) ➔ Spinal Cord ➔ Effector Hand Muscles

📊 Experimental Variables

Independent Variable Physical Exercise State (Resting vs Post-exercise)
Dependent Variable Ruler drop distance d (cm) & Calculated reaction time t (ms)
Controlled Variables Ruler drop height, visual focus point, release unpredictability
